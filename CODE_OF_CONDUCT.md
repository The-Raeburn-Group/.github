# Code of Conduct

Raeburn AI is committed to creating a welcoming, respectful, and productive open-source community.

## Our Standards

Positive behaviour includes:

- Being respectful and constructive
- Welcoming newcomers
- Giving and receiving feedback professionally
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Using inclusive language
- Respecting different backgrounds, skill levels, and perspectives

Unacceptable behaviour includes:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination
- Personal attacks or insults
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behaviour
- Publishing private information without permission
- Abusive, sexualised, or threatening language
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional open-source community

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject pull requests, or restrict participation where behaviour violates this code.

## Scope

This code applies across Raeburn AI repositories, discussions, issues, pull requests, documentation, and related community spaces.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainers through the official project channels.
