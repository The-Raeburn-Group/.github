# Code of Conduct

The Raeburn Group is committed to maintaining a professional, respectful and constructive environment across repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions and other community spaces associated with this GitHub organisation.

## Our standards

Positive behaviour includes:

- being respectful and constructive;
- welcoming people with different backgrounds and levels of experience;
- giving and receiving feedback professionally;
- focusing discussion on the work and the needs of the project;
- using inclusive language;
- respecting privacy, confidentiality and intellectual property.

Unacceptable behaviour includes:

- harassment, intimidation or discrimination;
- personal attacks or insults;
- trolling or deliberately disruptive behaviour;
- publishing private or confidential information without permission;
- abusive, sexualised or threatening language;
- conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional engineering or open-source community.

## Scope

This code applies across **The Raeburn Group** GitHub organisation wherever a repository does not publish a more specific code of conduct.

Individual projects may have different contribution models, licences and levels of public participation. Those differences do not change the expectation of professional conduct.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject pull requests, restrict participation or take other proportionate action where behaviour violates this code.

## Reporting

Concerns may be raised with the relevant repository maintainers or through the official Group contact route at **contact@theraeburngroup.com** where a private escalation is more appropriate.
